## Extension execution evidence

See verification-extension.md and RecoveryIT for executed additions: cases2–5 (lost commit/ack, concurrent complete,
storage outage),8/13/14/15 (process kill, lease takeover, stale publication and failed commit),20 (local
rejection/deletion policy),24 (single-primary restart, not HA failover),26/27 (deduplicated progress/outbox rollback),30
(deletion during encoding plus primary-bucket version erasure). Source abort/restart, legal holds, scan
infection/outage, body-length bounds and active-session GC are also exercised. Other cases remain proposed or partially
covered; no claim that all30 chaos scenarios ran is made.

## Local hardening execution evidence

[Current verification](verification-local-hardening.md) adds real-container checks for9 (encoder timeout/child
termination),11 (actual tmpfs ENOSPC),12 (cgroup OOM/reclaim),13/14 (cgroup-frozen writer plus takeover), and
scratch/legal-hold retention after crashes. Live EICAR, scanner outage/recovery and recorded engine/database evidence
passed. The local store/DB restart and primary erasure checks were rerun. Full malformed-media, arbitrary scanner
format/resource corpus, managed failover, regional faults, AWS/CDN and global workloads remain unexecuted.

Large-media additions: [executed evidence](verification-large-video.md) covers late-window failures, an overall scan
deadline, complete submission/hash evidence, constrained MP4 references/offsets, manual review of limited verdicts,
real10GiB resume/concurrent completion and an actual negative decoder corpus. This does not claim arbitrary
malware/exploit detection, complete native whole-file semantics or managed/cloud/global failure qualification.
